About Natasha

The Original Gajjar.

Natasha BBQ is built around fire. Our chicken, mishikaki, nundu and fish come off the charcoal grill, and our signature is the Original Gajjar Chicken.

Choose from starters, main dishes, sizzlers and sides, from chicken wings and lamb chops to red snapper and prawns, with fries on the side.

Sit down, open the table menu and order from your table.
